**v4.5 additions:** Publication-plan **velocity governance** (Tier 3) — match publishing rate to the site's baseline + editorial capacity, jitter the cadence, ramp new corpora gradually, never dump a sprint in one spike. This is the planning-side control for `algorithmic-authorship-gate` v1.3's R44 publish-velocity lens: a velocity spike vs baseline (≈10× historical) is a documented SpamBrain scaled-content-abuse signal, and there is no AI-text detector as a ranking factor (Ahrefs ~0.011 correlation across 600k pages) — the risk is corpus-level sameness/velocity, not authorship. A low Fingerprint Diffusion Score driven by D4 (velocity) is fixed here (throttle/jitter), not by rewriting. Pairs with `seo-blog-generator` v5.0's Step 4 batch-variance directive so a sprint is neither structurally nor temporally fingerprinted.
